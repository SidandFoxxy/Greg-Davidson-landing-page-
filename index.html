About

Performing music is in Greg Davidson’s blood. It pours from the heart — every song is a story shared with the room. Greg blends familiar favorites with heartfelt originals to create intimate, memorable sets. For booking: gregcdavidson@gmail.com.

More Than Music

Greg’s performances are an invitation — to sing, to listen, and to be part of a shared moment. He plays for the room, the story, and the people who gather to hear it.
